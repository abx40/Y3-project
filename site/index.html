Why the per-second signal looks flat

At one-second granularity the live signal is near chance for every backend (0.44–0.54). Ten-second windowing recovers F3Net to 0.659. The detector is not broken: single frames pulled out of a re-encoded, frame-dropped video call carry very little of the signal the model was trained on. That is the finding, and it is why the table above reports windows.
