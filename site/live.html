Read this before trusting a number

The project backends are the project's own fine-tuned detectors, exported to ONNX from the checkpoints used in the evaluation. Their measured test-set AUROC:

backendframe-level offline10 s window offline10 s window live
F3Net0.8370.8630.659
Xception0.7470.7950.609
EfficientNet-B40.7610.7820.562

The headline finding is the gap between the last two columns: each of these detectors loses accuracy once frames pass through a live video-call pipeline (F3Net drops 0.204). At one-second granularity all six project backends were near chance (0.44–0.54), which is why the verdict here uses a 10-second mean. A webcam in your room is further still from the training data, so expect these numbers to be worse here, not better. The ViT-base option is a public third-party image detector included to test the pipeline; it is not one of the project's backends. Treat every output as a demonstration that inference works, not as a verdict about a person.
