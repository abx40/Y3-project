Runs entirely in this browser tab; no video leaves your machine. Detectors: "Noise check" is a hand-written rule that flags a face with less camera noise than your camera normally has. The others are trained deepfake detectors from DeepfakeBench (trained on FaceForensics++), run on a face crop, with 15-check averaging. Each detector calibrates itself during its first 5 seconds: it watches how it scores your real face in the original feed and puts the alert threshold just above that. Drag the slider or press Recalibrate to change it. Preset faces are synthetic (people who do not exist), from the SFHQ dataset. The AI swap uses InsightFace's inswapper_128 network (non-commercial research licence), run on your GPU with WebGPU.
